PRODUCT 01 · LAUNCHING SOON

GSM Automatic
Pump Starter

A practical pump-control solution for agricultural applications, combining remote GSM operation with advanced pump and motor protection.

JEENU
PUMP
GSM CONTROLLER
WHY IT EXISTS

Less manual intervention. More control.

Farm pump operation can involve inconvenient site visits and exposure to electrical or operating faults. Jeenu Corporation is developing a controller that puts remote operation and protection into one practical system.

ConnectivityGSM / cellular
ApplicationAgricultural pump systems
Product stagePre-launch
Design focusAutomation + protection
FEATURE AREAS
01

Remote pump control

GSM-based operation intended for situations where the user is away from the pump.

02

Automatic starting

Designed to reduce repetitive manual switching and simplify daily operation.

03

Protection functions

Advanced protection features are being engineered around the electrical and operating conditions of agricultural pumps.

04

Status & fault awareness

Designed to provide useful information about pump operation and detected fault conditions.
