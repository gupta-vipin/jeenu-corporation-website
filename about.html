ABOUT JEENU CORPORATION

Engineering technology for the real farm.

Jeenu Corporation is a smart agriculture product engineering and manufacturing company focused on practical automation, monitoring and protection technologies for farmers, dealers and distributors.

OUR MISSION

Make useful agricultural technology practical.

We believe agricultural automation should be dependable, understandable and designed around actual field conditions. Our goal is to build products that reduce unnecessary manual work while improving equipment protection and control.

Our first product is a GSM-based automatic pump starter with advanced pump protection features. It is the beginning of a broader focus on connected agricultural systems.
