FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About Jeenu Corporation

What does Jeenu Corporation make?

Jeenu Corporation is a smart agriculture product engineering and manufacturing company. It develops practical automation and protection technology for farms. Its first product is a GSM-based automatic pump starter with advanced pump protection.

What is the GSM automatic pump starter?

A controller being developed to automate agricultural pumps and allow remote operation via GSM / cellular connectivity, with protection features engineered around agricultural pump operating conditions.

Is the pump starter available for sale yet?

No — it is currently pre-launch. Final specifications and supported protection functions will be published after product validation. Contact Jeenu Corporation for early-access information.

Who does Jeenu Corporation serve?

Farmers, dealers and distributors, with a longer-term focus on international customers.
